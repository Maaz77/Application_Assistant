Bring this job application to its application form, then stop.
Done means: the application's own fields (name, email, phone, resume, questions) are on screen and nothing covers them.
- Cookie or consent banner: choose the option that declines optional cookies, for example "Reject all", "I do not accept", "Decline" or "Only necessary".
- A pop-up between the job and the form, such as a job search safety reminder: choose the option that continues the application, for example "Continue applying".
- A job description page: click the button or link that starts the application, for example "Easy Apply", "Apply", "Apply now", "Apply for this job" or "Apply manually".
- Ignore job alerts, newsletter or talent-community sign-ups, search boxes, chat windows, "Save" buttons and other jobs. Their fields are not the application form.
- Never type anything. Never create an account or sign in. Never close, dismiss or discard the application.
